# Privacy Policy for Senior Simple Launcher

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Senior Simple Launcher ("the app") is a home-screen app for Android published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY].

## What the app stores
The app stores the following on your phone only, in the app's private storage:

- the emergency number you choose, and an optional family contact's name and phone number
- your text-size choice
- the list of favorite apps you add to "My Apps"

## What the app does NOT do
- It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal information.
- It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- It does not request internet access.
- It does not read your contacts, messages, photos or location.

## Other apps
When you tap Phone Call, Messages, Grandkids Photos, Emergency Help or an app in "My Apps", the
app opens another app on your phone (for example your dialer or messaging app). Those apps
have their own privacy practices.

## Emergency Help
Emergency Help opens your phone's dialer with a number already typed in. You place the call
yourself by pressing the call button. The app is not a monitored emergency service and does
not share your location.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. You can also clear it in Android
Settings > Apps > Senior Simple Launcher > Storage > Clear data.

## Children
The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
